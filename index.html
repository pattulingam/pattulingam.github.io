QA automation Featured

AI test case and test plan generator

A Python agent that turns requirements into QA deliverables. It reads a JIRA story with its acceptance criteria, comments, screenshots and linked Confluence documents, then generates manual test cases. A second mode builds a standard Integration Test Plan from a scope document and an estimate: scenarios and case counts come from the estimate, behaviour and expected results from the scope, and conflicts between the two are flagged. Output goes to Excel, JSON or Markdown, and the saved plan can be edited and rebuilt without calling the model. Works with OpenAI or Anthropic models, and ships with a Cursor skill.
