I work on TPP, a new business and underwriting platform that takes an application from case import through requirements management and underwriting to policy issue. AI for QA Featured

Client-specific AI test case generator

An LLM-powered agent that generates test cases and integration test plans for a specific insurance client, following that client's change-order process and standard plan format. It reads a JIRA story with its acceptance criteria, comments, screenshots and linked Confluence documents. For change requests it combines the scope document and the estimate: the estimate sets scenarios and case counts, the scope sets behavior and expected results, and conflicts between the two are flagged for review. Output goes to Excel, JSON or Markdown; the saved plan can be edited and rebuilt without another model call. Works with OpenAI or Anthropic models and ships with a Cursor skill.
